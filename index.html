FamyLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short: FamyLoop needs an account. Your check-ins, medications and game results are stored on your phone and backed up to your account in the cloud. If you join a family group, the group’s admins can see what you share with it. Premium AI features send a small set of ratings and counts, never your notes, symptoms or medication names, to an AI model. We show no ads and do not track you.

1. Who we are

FamyLoop (“the app”) is operated by Fuyama Hong Kong Limited (“we”, “us”). We decide why and how your information is used. Contact: famyloop@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Account details: email address, display name, account type (Senior or Caregiver), a personal code, and your password (held by Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it)To create and secure your account and let family members find and invite you
Health-related entries you make: daily check-ins (mood, energy, sleep, symptoms, notes), medications and their reminder times, and your responses to reminders (taken, snoozed, forgot)To run the app and, if you join a family group, to share them with that group

Your to-do list is never shared with your family group, but it is included in your personal cloud backup. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.

Health-related information is sensitive. You choose what to enter, and you can delete any entry at any time.

3. Where your data lives

  • On your phone: everything you enter is stored in a local database on your device.
  • Your cloud backup: when you are signed in, your check-ins, medications, reminder responses, game results, to-dos and daily tip are backed up to your account so you can restore them on a new phone. Only you can read your own backup.
  • Your family group: see section 4.
  • One phone at a time: FamyLoop does not sync between phones. If you sign in on a second phone, the app asks which phone to use and replaces that phone’s data with your latest backup.

4. Family groups

If you join a family group, a shared copy of your check-ins (including mood, symptoms and notes), medications, medication responses and game results is stored for the group. The group’s admins can see it. Other members cannot see your health entries. Your to-do list is never shared.

Joining needs the group’s invite code and an admin’s approval. You can leave at any time, and your shared copy is then removed from the group. Admins should only add people who have agreed, and must use what they see to care for that person.

5. AI features (Premium)

Premium includes AI-generated weekly summaries, trend notes, daily tips and nudge drafts. They run only when you or a group admin uses them. To produce them, the app sends a limited set of data through our server (a Cloudflare Worker) to Google’s Gemini AI model:

  • Weekly summary: display names and weekly figures such as the number of check-ins, average mood, energy and sleep ratings, how many doses were taken or missed, and games played.
  • Trend note: dated mood, energy and sleep ratings from your last 14 check-ins.
  • Daily tip: mood, energy and sleep ratings from your last 7 check-ins.
  • Nudge draft: the recipient’s display name and the topic the admin chose.

We never send your notes, symptoms or medication names to the AI model.

We currently use Google’s free Gemini tier. Under Google’s terms for that tier, Google may use what it receives to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. For this reason, please do not write personal or sensitive details in nudge topics. Summaries are cached in your account so they are not regenerated needlessly. They can be wrong and are not medical advice.

6. Voice to-do and the microphone

The Voice To-Do feature uses Android’s built-in speech recognition. FamyLoop does not record or store audio. It keeps only the text of your to-do. Depending on your phone, Android’s speech service (provided by Google or your phone maker) may process the audio on the device or on its servers under its own privacy policy.

7. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, exact alarms, start-on-boot and vibration: to deliver reminders on time and restore them after a restart.
  • Microphone: only for Voice To-Do, only when you tap the microphone.
  • Internet and network state: for sign-in, backup, family sync, AI features and subscriptions.
  • Billing: to offer Google Play subscriptions.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. We use these providers to run the app:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and cloud storage.
  • Google Play: payments and subscriptions. We never see your card details. Google tells the app which plan you hold.
  • Cloudflare: runs the server that passes AI requests on, and keeps usage counters.
  • Google (Gemini): generates AI text, as described in section 5.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. Members of your family group see what is described in section 4.

9. Security and international transfers

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to cloud data is restricted by account rules so users can only read what they are entitled to. No system is perfectly secure. Our providers operate data centres in several countries, so your information may be processed outside your own country, including in the United States.

10. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. You can delete your account in the app (Settings, then Delete account) or by email. Deleting your account removes your profile, cloud backup, invites and nudges, and your shared copy in any family group. Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically: cancel them in Google Play. Purchase records stay with Google Play, and our providers may keep technical logs or backups for a limited time. Full details, including how to delete only some of your data: Delete your FamyLoop account.

If a group’s creator deletes the whole group, each other member’s shared copy is removed the next time that member opens the app.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email famyloop@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis: we process account and subscription data to provide the service you asked for, and health-related information with your consent, which you give by entering it and agreeing to this policy. You can withdraw consent by deleting the data or your account.

12. Children

FamyLoop is for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

13. No advertising or tracking

FamyLoop contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking services, and it does not use the advertising ID.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. For significant changes we will tell you in the app.
